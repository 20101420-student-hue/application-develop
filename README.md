# My Favorite Movie: Inception

> "An idea is like a virus. Resilient. Highly contagious."

---

## Overview
**Inception** is a 2010 science fiction action film written and directed by *Christopher Nolan*. The story follows a professional thief who steals information by infiltrating the subconscious of his targets.

---

## Main Characters
* **Dom Cobb** – *The Extractor*
* **Arthur** – *The Point Man*
* **Ariadne** – *The Architect*
* **Eames** – *The Forger*

---

## Key Plot Themes
1. **Dream Sharing Technology**
2. **Subconscious Security**
3. **The Concept of Inception**
